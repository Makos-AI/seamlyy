# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Seamlyy MVP

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved for MVP Development
**Target Timeline:** 2-3 Months to Launch

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Objective

The Open Payments Art Platform is a streamlined digital marketplace designed to validate a novel creator economy model.

**Core Objective:** To validate that artists will utilize the platform to sell and display their artwork, and that viewers/collectors will adopt Open Payments for frictionless one-time purchases and micro-transactions (pay-to-view access).

## 2. Target Audience & User Roles

The platform serves a two-sided market:

* **Artists (Supply):** Creators seeking alternative, low-friction methods to monetize their digital and physical artwork while building an audience.
* **Viewers/Collectors (Demand):** Art enthusiasts and patrons willing to make direct purchases or pay small micro-amounts to access exclusive, gated content.

---

## 3. Scope Definition

### 3.1. In Scope for MVP (V1)

* User Authentication & Wallet Integration
* Role-based User Profiles (Artist vs. Viewer)
* Artwork Upload and Inventory Management
* Basic Discovery (Chronological Feeds, Hardcoded Categories, Simple Search)
* Payments: One-Time Purchases (Fixed Price) via Open Payments
* Payments: Pay-to-View Micro-transactions for Premium Galleries via Open Payments

### 3.2. Strictly Out of Scope (Do Not Build for V1)

To ensure a rapid 2-3 month launch, the following features are explicitly excluded:

* Auctions, bidding, or "Make an Offer" workflows.
* Streaming payments (pay-per-minute/time-based Web Monetization).
* Monthly recurring subscriptions or Patronage models.
* Curator roles or user-generated public collections/boards.
* Complex algorithmic feeds (e.g., "Trending," "Recommended for You").
* In-app messaging (Users will rely on provided social links/email).
* Advanced Analytics or seller dashboards.

## 4. Core Functional Requirements

### Epic 1: Authentication, Onboarding & Profiles

* **1.1 User Registration:** Users must be able to sign up via a simple Email/Password flow or Google OAuth.
* **1.2 Wallet Setup:** Both Artists and Viewers must be able to link their Open Payments wallet pointer (e.g., `$[wallet.example.com/alice](https://wallet.example.com/alice)`). This is required to send or receive funds.
* **1.3 Universal Profile Data:** All user profiles must display a Profile Picture, Bio, Location, Social Links, and Follower/Following counts.
* **1.4 Artist Profile UI:**
* **Tab 1 - Public Portfolio:** Free-to-view feed of artworks. Items here can be marked as "Not for Sale" or have a "Fixed Price".
* **Tab 2 - Premium Exhibitions:** A section displaying locked/blurred galleries that require a pay-to-view fee.


* **1.5 Viewer Profile UI:**
* **Tab 1 - Saved Artworks:** A private mood board of liked/saved items.
* **Tab 2 - Purchased/Unlocked:** A digital receipt book providing persistent access to purchased artworks and unlocked premium galleries.



### Epic 2: Artwork & Gallery Management (Artist Flow)

* **2.1 Upload Module:** Capability to upload high-resolution image files.
* **2.2 Metadata Entry:** Form fields for Title, Description, Medium, Dimensions, and Year Created.
* **2.3 Monetization Toggles (Crucial):** Upon upload, the artist must select a monetization route:
* **Option A (Standard Listing):** Artist sets status to either `[Not for Sale]` or `[Fixed Price]` (e.g., $150).
* **Option B (Premium Exhibition):** Artist groups 3–10 images into a single "Gallery" and sets a micro-transaction access fee (e.g., $1.00).



### Epic 3: Discovery & Browsing

* **3.1 Home Feed:** A simple chronological feed displaying "Recently Added" standard artworks and newly published Premium Galleries.
* **3.2 Categories:** Hardcoded filtering options (e.g., Digital Art, Oil Paintings, Photography, Sculptures).
* **3.3 Search:** A basic text search querying the database for Artist Names or Artwork Titles.

### Epic 4: The Payments Engine (Open Payments)

* **4.1 Method 1: One-Time Purchases (Fixed Price)**
* System triggers an Open Payments quote when a viewer clicks "Buy Artwork".
* System prompts the viewer to authorize the transfer for the full amount.
* Upon success, funds are transferred directly to the Artist's wallet pointer.
* System updates the artwork's database status and UI to "Sold".


* **4.2 Method 2: Pay-to-View (Micro-transactions)**
* System displays a blurred/locked cover for Premium Galleries.
* Viewer clicks "Unlock Exhibition for $X.XX".
* System triggers a seamless Open Payments micro-transaction.
* Upon success, the system immediately unblurs/unlocks the gallery assets for that specific user's session and account.

## 5. Key User Flows

### Flow 1: The Pay-to-View Unlock (Micro-transaction)

1. Viewer navigates to the "Photography" category.
2. Viewer selects an artist and views their profile.
3. Viewer notices a locked gallery in Tab 2 titled *"Midnight in Tokyo - $1 Access"*.
4. Viewer clicks the **"Unlock"** button.
5. System prompts: *"Confirm $1.00 payment via Open Payments?"* -> Viewer clicks **Confirm**.
6. Open Payments processes the transaction in seconds.
7. Gallery unlocks. The viewer can now view high-res images, read the artist's commentary, and save individual pieces to their profile.

### Flow 2: The One-Time Purchase (Physical/Digital Art)

1. Viewer scrolls the chronological Home Feed.
2. Viewer sees an Oil Painting available for $300.
3. Viewer clicks **"Buy Artwork"**.
4. Viewer fills out a basic text form with shipping details (for physical goods).
5. Viewer authorizes the payment; Open Payments handles the $300 transfer to the artist.
6. Artist receives an automated email notification containing the buyer's shipping address and transaction confirmation. UI updates to "Sold".

## 6. Technical Architecture & Stack

To support fast UI updates and secure gating of premium content, the following stack is recommended:

* **Frontend:** React, Next.js, or Vue.
* *Rationale:* Ideal for fast, responsive image loading, seamless navigation, and dynamic state changes (e.g., instantly swapping blurred placeholders with high-res images upon payment confirmation).


* **Backend:** Node.js/Express or Python/Django.
* *Rationale:* Capable of securely handling user authentication, processing database relationships (follows/saves), and securely communicating with the Open Payments APIs (Grant Negotiation and Authorization Protocol).


* **Database:** PostgreSQL (Relational) or MongoDB (NoSQL).
* *Required Entities:* `Users`, `Artworks`, `Galleries`, `Transactions`, and `Follows/Saves`.


* **Storage:** AWS S3 or Cloudinary.
* *Rationale:* Crucial for handling image compression, generating public thumbnails, and—most importantly—securely gating premium images. High-res images must be locked behind pre-signed URLs so they cannot be accessed via direct URL inspection before payment is verified.


* **Payments Infrastructure:** Interledger Protocol (ILP) and Open Payments APIs.
